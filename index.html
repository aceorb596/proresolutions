How does the $125 stairwell offer work?
Stairwell cleaning is offered at $125 per clean. Share the stairwell location and condition so Pro Resolutions can confirm the scope and access details before scheduling.
When does the $1,250 building rate apply?
The $1,250 per-building rate applies when you order cleaning for 5 or more buildings at a time. A 5-building cleaning order totals $6,250 before any separately quoted bird protection. The final cleaning scope is agreed in your quote.
Is bird protection included in the building price?
No. Bird protection is an optional addition, offered at a discount with orders of 5 or more buildings. The protection approach, price, and discount are confirmed in the quote.
Can I request fewer than five buildings?
The advertised building bundle starts at five buildings. For a smaller job, use the stairwell offer where it fits, or discuss the property with the team for a separate quote.
